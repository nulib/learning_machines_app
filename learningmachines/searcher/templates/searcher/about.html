Interpreting the Model Visualization

The Model of Models (MoM) integrates machine learning, data visualization, and information retrieval to support transdisciplinary research on large unstructured datasets.

Merging humanistic methods with technical innovations, the system encourages collaboration among scholars from various fields by combining algorithms into a network-based visualization interface, allowing non-technical users to engage with the data.

Initially designed and developed at the University of Cincinnati with funding generously provided by the Mellon Foundation>, MoM is now maintained by Northwestern University Libraries.

The Topic-Cluster Chart columns include the Topic-Cluster ID (this is a label only and does not denote meaning), Docs (the number of documents from the dataset that relate to that topic-cluster), Topics (the number of topics form that topic-cluster), Terms (the top 5 terms from that topic-cluster)
